Verum Wallet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

UTIL ATIVIDADES WEB LTDA, CNPJ 47.509.953/0001-43, and its affiliates ("Verum," "we," "our," or "ours") respect your privacy and are strictly committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, and use the information generated when you interact with the Verum Wallet application, our website, and related services.

As a self-custody software native to the Solana blockchain, we do not have access to, collect, or store your private keys (Seed Phrase), spending passwords, or the assets held in your wallet. You have 100% control over your assets.

By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our application.

2. Who We Are and Contact Us

Data Controller: UTIL ATIVIDADES WEB LTDA, CNPJ 47.509.953/0001-43 ("Verum"), headquartered in Brazil, is the legal entity responsible for the Verum ecosystem and the controller of the personal data described in this policy, under the terms of the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13.709/2018 – "LGPD").

Data Protection Officer (DPO / Encarregado — LGPD, Art. 41): Verum's Data Protection Officer is the channel of communication between you, Verum, and the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD). You may contact the DPO to exercise any of the rights described in Section 9, to ask questions about this policy, or to report an incident, at: contato@verumcrypto.com (subject line: "DPO — Data Protection"). We respond to requests within up to 15 (fifteen) days of receipt.

Third-Party Links and Integrations: Our interface includes connectivity with third-party swap providers and protocols — Jupiter, Raydium, and ChangeNOW. Clicking on these links or using these integrations means interacting with independent platforms, which act as independent controllers of the data you provide to them. We do not control these services and are not responsible for their policies. We strongly recommend reading the Privacy Policies of these partners.

3. The Data We Collect

Our principle is minimal data collection. However, for the application to function properly, we may process the following categories:

Information you provide to us: When you contact our support team, report a bug, or participate in community surveys, we may collect your name, email address, and the details of your message.

Technical and Usage Data: Whenever you use Verum Wallet, we may automatically collect system information. This includes IP address (anonymized when possible), device type, operating system, and interaction metrics (which screens were accessed, clicks, and errors in the app). This is vital for keeping the application fast and secure.

Blockchain Data (Public): The Solana blockchain is a public and immutable ledger. Your wallet address (public key) and the transactions made on it are visible to anyone in the world. We do not link your public wallet address to your real identity (name, CPF, etc.) unless you send us this information directly in a support request.

Financial and KYC Data (Not collected by us): As we are only the software interface, we do not perform card processing, PIX, fiat on-ramp/off-ramp, or KYC (Know Your Customer) processes. Swaps performed through Jupiter and Raydium are on-chain operations that involve only public blockchain data. Where a cross-chain swap is executed by ChangeNOW and ChangeNOW requires any verification under its own AML/KYC rules, you provide that data directly to ChangeNOW, within its own environment, and ChangeNOW collects and stores it as an independent controller. Verum does not receive, view, or store this data; at most we receive an operational status (e.g. "swap completed" or "swap rejected") so that the interface can be updated.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the limited data we possess exclusively to:

  • Ensure the operation, technical security, and stability of Verum Wallet.
  • Analyze usage trends to improve the user experience (UX) and payment flow in partner merchants.
  • Provide efficient technical support.
  • Prevent malicious activities, fraud, and protect the integrity of our software.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We only process personal data when a legal basis under Article 7 of the LGPD applies (and, for data subjects in the European Economic Area, under Article 6 of the GDPR). The bases we rely on are:

  • Performance of a contract (LGPD, Art. 7, V; GDPR, Art. 6(1)(b)): to operate the application, enable the functionalities you request, route your request to the applicable swap provider, and provide technical support.
  • Legitimate interests (LGPD, Art. 7, IX; GDPR, Art. 6(1)(f)): to ensure the security and stability of the software, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the user experience through aggregated usage metrics. We balance these interests against your rights and freedoms, and you may object at any time (Section 9).
  • Compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation (LGPD, Art. 7, II; GDPR, Art. 6(1)(c)): to retain records required by law and to respond to lawful orders from competent authorities.
  • Consent (LGPD, Art. 7, I; GDPR, Art. 6(1)(a)): for optional analytics and marketing communications, where applicable. Consent is freely given and may be withdrawn at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.
  • Exercise of rights in proceedings (LGPD, Art. 7, VI): where necessary for the exercise of rights in judicial, administrative, or arbitral proceedings.

6. Data Sharing

Verum will never sell your personal data. We only share strictly necessary information in the following scenarios:

  • Swap Providers (Jupiter, Raydium, ChangeNOW): swaps offered through Verum Wallet are executed by third-party providers and protocols. Jupiter and Raydium are decentralized on-chain protocols: the data involved is limited to public blockchain data (wallet addresses, assets, and amounts). Cross-chain swaps are executed by ChangeNOW; when you start such an operation, any data requested by ChangeNOW is provided directly to it. ChangeNOW acts as an independent controller of this data and determines on its own how long and for what purposes it keeps it, under its own Privacy Policy, available at changenow.io. Verum may share with these providers only minimal technical data needed to initiate and track the operation — such as the wallet addresses involved, the asset and amount requested, the operation identifier, and basic session data (locale/IP for availability and fraud-prevention purposes). Verum does not send these providers your identity documents, and does not receive from them the documents or verification data you provide to them.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Companies that provide us with cloud hosting services, data analytics, and customer support tools, operating as processors under strict confidentiality and data processing agreements, and only on our instructions.
  • Legal Obligations: We may share limited information we possess if required by law, court order, or applicable legal process in our jurisdiction of operation.
  • Corporate Transactions: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or corporate restructuring, data may be transferred to the successor entity, which shall remain bound by this policy.

7. International Transfer of Data

Verum operates with cloud infrastructure and partners located outside Brazil. This means that your personal data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in other countries — in particular the European Union and the United States (where part of our hosting, analytics, and third-party provider infrastructure is located).

These international transfers are carried out in accordance with Articles 33 to 36 of the LGPD, relying on at least one of the following grounds:

  • transfer to a country that provides an adequate level of personal data protection (LGPD, Art. 33, I) — as is the case for the countries of the European Economic Area;
  • contractual guarantees, by means of standard contractual clauses or specific contractual clauses that ensure compliance with the principles and the rights of the data subject (LGPD, Art. 33, II);
  • necessity of the transfer for the performance of a contract to which you are a party, or for preliminary procedures related to a contract at your request (LGPD, Art. 33, V) — this is the ground applicable when you choose to transact with a third-party swap provider such as ChangeNOW;
  • compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation (LGPD, Art. 33, III).

For data subjects in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, transfers to third countries are made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an applicable adequacy decision. You may request further information about the safeguards adopted by contacting our DPO.

8. User Security and Responsibility

The security of your assets is based on the advanced cryptography of the Solana network and the biometric capabilities of your device (Face ID/Touch ID).

As Verum is a non-custodial wallet, the responsibility for storing your Recovery Phrase (12 to 24 words) in a secure, offline location rests solely with you. If you lose your Recovery Phrase or give it to scammers, Verum has no power, authority, or technical means to recover your funds, as your assets are on the blockchain and not on our servers.

9. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Article 18 of the LGPD, you may at any time, free of charge, request from Verum:

  • Confirmation of processing — to know whether we process data concerning you;
  • Access — to obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correction — of incomplete, inaccurate, or out-of-date data;
  • Anonymisation, blocking, or deletion — of data that is unnecessary, excessive, or processed in breach of the LGPD;
  • Portability — transfer to another service or product provider, upon express request;
  • Deletion of data processed on the basis of consent — except in the cases of lawful retention provided in Article 16 of the LGPD;
  • Information about sharing — the public and private entities with which we have shared your data;
  • Information about the consequences of refusing consent;
  • Withdrawal of consent — at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand;
  • Objection — to processing carried out on a legal basis other than consent, where there is non-compliance with the LGPD;
  • Review of automated decisions — where applicable.

For data subjects covered by the GDPR, the equivalent rights of Articles 15 to 22 apply, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

How to exercise them: write to our DPO at contato@verumcrypto.com. We may ask for additional information to confirm your identity before fulfilling the request — solely to protect you. We respond within up to 15 (fifteen) days. You also have the right to file a complaint with the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

Important limitation: we can only act on data that is in our possession. Requests regarding any data you provide directly to a third-party swap provider — such as verification data requested by ChangeNOW — must be addressed directly to that provider, which is the controller of that data. We do not hold it and cannot alter or delete it. If you contact us, we will indicate the appropriate channel.

10. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Support records and communications: up to 2 (two) years after the matter is closed;
  • Technical, security, and access logs: up to 6 (six) months, as a general rule, or longer where a security incident is under investigation;
  • Aggregated and anonymised usage data: retained indefinitely, as it no longer identifies you;
  • Data subject to a legal retention obligation: for the period required by the applicable law, even after a deletion request (LGPD, Art. 16).

11. Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological or regulatory changes. We will notify users of significant changes through notices within the Verum Wallet application itself.
